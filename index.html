Hi, I'm Yuvaraj — the person behind YuvaSukuna. I build custom explainer animations for SaaS products that actually care about how their product is perceived.

I work in Adobe After Effects, frame-by-frame. Every transition, easing curve, and UI detail is intentional. I don't use templates because your product deserves to feel like itself — not like everything else on the internet.

My process is built around trust: you approve the script before I touch the timeline, milestones are shared weekly, and you get the raw source files at the end. No black boxes, no guessing.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is your turnaround time?
Typically 2–3 weeks from script approval to final delivery, depending on project scope.
Do I get the raw After Effects project files?
Yes. You receive fully organized source files (.aep) alongside the final export — no lock-in.
How does your pricing work?
Fixed pricing per project, agreed upfront. No surprise add-ons.
Can I request revisions?
Revisions are built into the process. We iterate at storyboard stage before animation begins to keep changes efficient.
